Symm Yield Guarantee · Application-based

Symm stands behind qualified operations.

The Symm Yield Guarantee is our commitment to growers who run the Symm program as directed, on operations we’ve reviewed and approved. It is application-based: every operation is reviewed individually by a Symm Field Advisor before enrollment, and full terms are shared once your application is approved. Apply below.

How it works

01

Apply

Tell us about your operation. It takes a few minutes and helps our team understand your fit for the program.

02

Review

A Symm Field Advisor reviews your application against the program’s qualification criteria.

03

Discuss terms

Qualified operations receive a call to walk through eligibility and the specifics of the guarantee for their acres.

04

Enroll

Approved growers enroll and run the Symm program under the terms outlined for their operation.

What we look for

An active operation

You run a working farm and can apply the Symm program to a defined set of acres this season.

A decision-maker

You own or help manage the operation and can commit to following the program on your fields.

Program fit

Your crops, geography, and scale line up with the operations our Field Advisors currently support.

A willingness to follow the protocol

You’re prepared to apply the Symm program as directed across the guaranteed acres.

What happens after you apply

A Symm Field Advisor reviews every application individually. Qualified operations hear back with next steps and a conversation about whether the guarantee is a fit for their acres. Pricing and full program terms are shared directly with qualified applicants and are not published on this site.
